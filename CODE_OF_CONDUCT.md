# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can disagree about code
without being unpleasant about people. Everyone who takes part — maintainers,
contributors, users filing issues — is expected to keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the change, not the person. "This transition is unguarded" is
  useful; "you clearly do not understand state machines" is not.
- Assume good faith and ask before concluding.
- Give the smallest reproduction you can and the exact command you ran.
- Accept that a maintainer may reject a contribution with a reason. A rejection
  is a decision about scope, not a judgement of you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer at **xuvigan@users.noreply.github.com**.
Reports are read by the maintainer only. Consequences range from a private
warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition. The reporter
will be told that the report arrived and what happened, without exposing other
people's details.
